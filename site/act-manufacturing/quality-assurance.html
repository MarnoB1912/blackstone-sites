Quality assurance

Every batch tested. Every batch traceable.

Our quality process is designed to reduce raw-material variation and verify product consistency before any material leaves the premises.
Discuss a testing requirement

Raw materials

Virgin inputs, controlled variation.

We use virgin raw materials rather than reclaimed inputs, importing where required, to minimise the effects of raw-material variation and keep products consistent year after year.

Controlled production

Deliberately managed batches.

Production is kept to a maximum of 250 tonnes per month and batch sizes are kept to a minimum. Even orders below one tonne are individually tested.

Certification

A certificate with every batch.

A test certificate is supplied for every batch. Technical Data Sheets and Safety Data Sheets are available on request for any product in the range.

Testing

The tests behind each certificate.

Samples are cast and dried at 110°C to determine bulk density and cold crushing strength, with further tests on request.

Bulk Density

A measure of mass relative to volume, used as an important consistency and product-characterisation indicator.

Cold Crushing Strength

A measure of compressive strength under specified cold-testing conditions.

Permanent Linear Change

Used where fired dimensional change forms part of the product or purchase specification.

ASTM Abrasion Testing

Performed on request and for selected ACTCHEM products where abrasion performance forms part of the requirement.
